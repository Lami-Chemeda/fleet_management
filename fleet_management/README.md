# Fleet & Fuel Management Pro

Transform your logistics with **Fleet & Fuel Management Pro**, the most comprehensive and scalable fleet and fuel management module built natively for Odoo 18. Move beyond spreadsheets and fully integrate your logistics operations into your ERP.

## Key Features

### ⛽ Advanced Fuel Management
Control every drop of fuel and eliminate theft.
- **Strict Fuel Quotas**: Allocate specific fuel quotas per vehicle or department.
- **Fuel Requests**: Drivers can submit fuel requests tied to current odometer readings.
- **Pump Station Logs**: Log station receipts and track fuel efficiency (L/100km).
- **Cost Allocation**: Seamlessly tie fuel expenses into your Odoo accounting system.

### 📍 GPS & Vehicle Tracking
- **Location Logging**: Ingest Latitude, Longitude, and Speed data from external hardware or apps into Odoo.
- **History View**: Track historical GPS logs directly from the vehicle dashboard.

### 🛣️ Trip Dispatching
- **Approval Workflows**: Multi-level managerial approval for dispatching.
- **Intelligent Allocation**: Smart vehicle assignment based on seat capacity and availability.

### 🔧 Automated Maintenance
- Prevent critical breakdowns with predictive alerts based on time or mileage.
- Create maintenance requests, approve them, and send them to external vendor garages.
- Automatically track maintenance invoices.

### 🏆 Driver Scorecards & KPIs
- Gamify your fleet! Drivers are evaluated out of 100 based on fuel efficiency, punctuality, and incident reports.

## Installation
1. Place the `fleet_management` directory inside your Odoo `addons` path.
2. Restart your Odoo server.
3. Update the App List and install **Fleet & Fuel Management Pro**.

## Configuration
- Navigate to **Fleet > Configuration** to set up Fuel Types, Departments, and Maintenance Vendors.
- Assign standard employees as "Fleet Drivers" from the HR Employee form.
